📖 Read the story اقرأ القصة

Billy woke up at 4:30 on Monday morning. He got dressed quickly, ate breakfast, and left his house with his bicycle and his dog, Rocky. It was still dark, and the moon was shining.

Billy cycled to the shop. He saw two big bags of newspapers. He looked at the map and delivered newspapers to every house. He put five newspapers through the letter box of the big white house.

Everyone was asleep. The only person Billy saw was the milkman. Suddenly, Rocky saw a fox and started barking loudly! “Shh, Rocky! I will lose my job!” said Billy. Rocky stopped barking.

It took Billy an hour and a half to deliver all the papers. He went home and went back to bed. He was tired, but he was happy. He thought about the money and fell asleep.
